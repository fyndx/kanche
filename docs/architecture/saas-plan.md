# Hosted service direction

Self-service onboarding, a developer console, and organization access management
are planned capabilities. Their contracts and delivery sequence remain under
review; they are not implemented by the current login slice.

See the [public roadmap](v1-roadmap.md) for the high-level direction and the
[integration overview](../features/integration.md) for available behavior.
Detailed product proposals and delivery tracking are maintained privately.
